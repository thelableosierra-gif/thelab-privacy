Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

THE LAB (“the app”) is a coaching app by Leo Sierra and María Claudia Sierra, published by THE LAB (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Profile details you enter: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number and birthdate.

  • Assessment answers: the text you type or dictate while completing the Purpose Assessment, your selections, and the results created from them (such as your purpose statement and mantra).

  • Preferences: your language, your notification choice, and the program categories you select.

  • Device notification ID: if you allow notifications, a device identifier is used by our notification provider, OneSignal, to deliver messages.

  • Microphone and speech: if you tap the microphone button, your device’s speech recognition turns your voice into text. We do not record or store audio.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or payment card details inside the app. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the app, save your progress, and show your results and recommended programs.

  • To generate your assessment results, your answers are sent securely to our server, which uses an AI service to write the reflection text. Answers are used only to produce your results.

  • To contact you about THE LAB programs, sessions, and updates, if you gave us your email or phone number.

  • To send notifications you have allowed.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and assessment progress are stored on your device. Information you submit through forms (for example to book a session or join a waitlist) is sent to us and to the service that hosts the form. We keep it only as long as needed to serve you.

Third parties

  • OneSignal: push notifications.

  • Calendly: booking a coaching session (opens in a web page).

  • Stripe: payment for 1:1 coaching programs and guides. Payment happens on Stripe’s secure web page in your browser; we never see your card number.

  • YouTube: videos in the app are played from YouTube and follow YouTube’s privacy policy.

  • Hosting and AI providers: used to process assessment results as described above.

Your choices

  • You can turn notifications off in your device settings or in the app.

  • You can delete your profile inside the app (More, then Profile, then delete). This clears the data stored on your device.

  • To ask us to delete data we hold about you, or to see what we have, email privacy@jointhelab.org and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
